# Spatial infection and deep-terrain pressure — 2.34

Terrain grayness and darkness now use a continuous world-space field. Each known infected chunk contributes a circular source at its center. Its source strength also grows its radius, from four to twenty-two blocks. The nine nearby sources add together and saturate at one. Young centers remain separate, intermediate stages grow bridges, and mature adjacent centers form a shared plateau. The same rule applies independently to grayness and blackness. A source also shades its soft fringe across the immediate neighboring chunks, without generating those chunks or altering server contamination there.

The field is evaluated per terrain fragment. A small RGBA texture stores source strengths, updated with the server snapshot. A camera-relative transform keeps floating-point coordinates small, including negative and distant world coordinates. Terrain transparency order, fog, lightmap and non-terrain rendering retain their previous behavior. Active shader packs keep the existing fallback behavior.

Darkness uses elapsed seconds instead of whole-minute steps. The existing `blackeningMinutes` setting remains valid, including previously customized values.

After local infection age exceeds `infectionDamageAfter` (default 1200 seconds), that infected column damages active survival exiles on any floor. Default damage is one health point (half a heart) every two seconds. It bypasses armor but uses normal magic-protection rules. Creative mode, ordinary spectator mode, cured players and pocket shelter retain their exclusions. Optional visual effects and brief infection symptoms do not control this damage. The area's maturity is persistent; leaving, returning or reloading does not restart its age. The damage amount and interval are available in the infection settings; zero damage disables it.

Ordinary ground pursuers that are more than twenty vertical blocks away, have no sight of their target and repeatedly fail to find a path are retired after thirty seconds of detected separation. Path checks run at most once per ten seconds per eligible mob, so total delay can reach roughly forty seconds. Retirement drops no loot or experience and frees population slots for normal appearances near the player's new floor. Trial mobs, manual test mobs, Vex and reachable ground mobs are retained. Legal spawn positions and the total population limits still apply; the terrain hazard provides pressure even when caves offer no legal positions.

Validation without launching Minecraft: production shader pixel tests for radial gradients, clean terrain, intermediate bridges, merged blackness and chunk seams; existing terrain/fog/alpha/FBO and convection tests; maturity, save/reload, damage, cave-depth and stranded-clock regressions; existing progression, encounter and archive checks.
